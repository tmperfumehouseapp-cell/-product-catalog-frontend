Add a shop banner

Photo carousel on the shop page, between the category bar and the Filter row. Multiple banners auto-slide every 4 seconds with dots; one banner shows as a single photo. Best size: wide images around 1200×480 (5:2 ratio).
Priority on the shop page: story banners (when that story is tapped) → category banners (for the selected category) → general banners.
Heights apply to the whole carousel (all banners), saved together with any banner.
